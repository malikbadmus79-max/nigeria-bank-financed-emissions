### Notes on each source (from the documents opened in Phase 0)

(i) National GHG inventory, UNFCCC. Nigeria's First Biennial Transparency Report (BTR1), dated 26 December 2024 (PDF p. 2), file `data/raw/intensity/Nigeria_BTR1.pdf` (134 pages), from https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Nigeria%20BTR1%20.pdf. The executive summary (PDF p. 16) states that the inventory covers the time series 2000 to 2022, uses the 2019 Refinement to the 2006 IPCC Guidelines and covers CO2, CH4 and N2O; HFCs and SF6 were not estimated. Table 2.10, "Summary of greenhouse gas emissions and removals in accordance with the common reporting table 10 emission trends" (PDF pp. 54-62), gives annual values in kt CO2e for 2000-2022 by IPCC category, including 1.A.1 energy industries, 1.A.2 manufacturing industries and construction, 1.A.3 transport, 1.A.4 other sectors, 1.B fugitive emissions (1.B.2 oil and natural gas), 2.A-2.C mineral, chemical and metal industry, 3.A-3.H agriculture, 4 LULUCF and 5.A-5.D waste. Printed totals on PDF p. 62 for 2022: 328,496.23 kt CO2e without LULUCF and 554,095.14 kt CO2e with LULUCF. Extraction difficulty: the table is split over nine pages in three year-blocks, and the year header on PDF p. 56 is printed as "2014 2016 2016". Licence: PDF p. 3 reads "Copyright 2024 by National Council on Climate Change ... All rights reserved"; no open-data licence is stated. The companion National Inventory Document (NID), dated 24 December 2024 (PDF p. 2), 265 pages, file `data/raw/intensity/Nigeria_BTR1_NID_2024.pdf`, from https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Nigeria%20BTR1%20NID%202024.pdf, was downloaded by the check script with Python `requests` (an earlier `curl` request had received a bot-protection page). Its list of tables (PDF p. 18 onward) includes category-level emission tables and activity data, for example "Table 3.9. Emissions (kt CO2 e) for Energy Industries (2000-2022)", "Table 3.18. Emissions (kt CO2 e) for Manufacturing Industries and Construction for selected years", "Table 3.25. Emissions (kt CO2 e) by sub-category for Transport for selected years" and activity-data tables for 2000-2022. The NID carries the same "All rights reserved" copyright notice (PDF p. 3). The BTR1 file was first obtained through a web-fetch tool after `curl` was blocked; the script re-uses that local copy.

(ii) NBS GDP by economic activity. "Rebasing of Gross Domestic Product (GDP): Key Highlights" (15 pages), file `data/raw/intensity/nbs_microdata_147_1256.pdf`, from https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/147/download/1256. The annex gives GDP at current basic prices (PDF p. 7) and at 2019 constant basic prices (PDF p. 8), plus percentage distributions (PDF pp. 9-10), for 2019-2024, by activity (e.g. 1 crop production, 2 livestock, 3 forestry, 4 fishing, 5 crude petroleum and natural gas, 6 coal mining, ..., 9 oil refining, 10 cement, 11 food, beverage and tobacco). Base year 2019 (PDF p. 2). 2025 is not covered by this document; press coverage reports an NBS Q4 2025 GDP release in February 2026, but the NBS document itself was not located or opened in Phase 0. No licence statement was found in the document. The NBS e-library (nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary) returned HTTP 500 to a scripted request at the time of the check.

(iii) Climate Watch. The public API (https://www.climatewatchdata.org/api/v1/data/historical_emissions) returned Nigeria series without registration; coverage by data source is in the table above and the raw JSON is saved in `data/raw/intensity/`. The "Climate Watch" source runs 1990-2023 with sectors including Electricity/Heat, Manufacturing/Construction, Transportation, Building, Fugitive Emissions, Industrial Processes, Agriculture, Waste and LULUCF; PIK runs to 2024 with IPCC top-level sectors; UNFCCC_NAI covers only 1994-2000 for Nigeria. Licence: the climatewatchdata.org permissions page renders through JavaScript and could not be read by script; search results attribute CC BY 4.0 to WRI's Climate Watch datasets, but this was not confirmed on an opened page.

(iv) PCAF emission factor database. PCAF Database information sheet dated 3 September 2025, file `data/raw/intensity/PCAF-2025Database-2Pager-R2.pdf`, from https://carbonaccountingfinancials.com/files/database/PCAF-2025Database-2Pager-R2.pdf. Page 1 states the database is "available to PCAF Signatories and Accredited Partners" and is accessed by emailing the database team for login details. Page 2 states that economic (revenue-based) emission factors for business loans and unlisted equity are based on the CEDA environmentally extended input-output table, with a map legend distinguishing country-specific data from a "Rest of World" proxy; whether Nigeria is country-specific or proxy could not be read from the sheet. The PCAF "join" page (https://carbonaccountingfinancials.com/en/join-pcaf) describes the database as a resource for signatories and accredited partners and does not state a fee. No route for access by a non-signatory researcher was found.
